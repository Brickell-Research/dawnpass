how to read this

  • swell — the bearing waves come from. PAG works on W/SW swell; N swell barely registers.
  • wind — the bearing wind comes from. PAG faces west, so east winds are offshore (clean) and west winds are onshore (blown out).
  • tide — observed level + trend, plus the next high/low time. Most Gulf longboard windows happen mid-tide on the drop.
  • score — 0-10 composite of swell, period, direction, wind. 0 skip · 3 marginal · 5 fun-sized · 7 go now · 9 fires.

how we score it

what I look at

Four inputs: wave height, period, swell direction, wind. Weighted sum, capped at 10, with a verdict (skip / marginal / fun-sized / go now / fires). Wind carries the most. The right swell with the wrong wind isn't surfable, but a soft swell with offshore wind can still be a day.

what "go now" looks like at PAG

Wave height in the longboard sweet spot, period over 7 seconds, swell wrapping in close to due west, and light offshore wind. Fires is all four at once. Florida Gulf longboard break, don't expect it often.

the kill switches

Wave too small, period under ~4 seconds (wind chop, not waves), or onshore wind over 12 knots. Any one vetoes the score regardless of the rest. The score block names which one fired.

the five-day caveat

Outlook runs five days. The wave model (GFS-Wave) loses skill past day three, so days 4 and 5 are faded out and tagged less certain. Useful for spotting a wind shift coming Friday, not for booking the weekend.

what I'm not pretending

Thresholds are taste, tuned by reading the surf science that exists and watching what PAG actually does. One spot. Tide is shown but not weighted yet, waiting on rated sessions to calibrate. No scraped sources, no AI in the scoring math. Whole model is a few hundred lines of Gleam in the repo.
